(CHATURANGA)

ROYAL SANDALWOOD ASHTAPADA ENGINE

Welcome to the ancient Indian battlefield. Experience the common ancestor of modern chess on an 8x8 Ashtapada board.

Rules of War

⚜️ Raja (King): Moves 1 square in any direction.

📜 Mantri (Minister): Moves 1 square diagonally.

🐘 Gaja (Elephant): Moves 2 squares diagonally (can jump).

🐎 Ashva (Horse): Moves in an L-shape (Knight equivalent).

🛕 Ratha (Chariot): Moves straight horizontally or vertically.

⚔️ Padati (Pawn): Moves 1 step forward, captures diagonally.

The Heritage

Developed during the Gupta Empire, Chaturanga represents the four arms of the ancient Indian military: infantry, cavalry, elephantry, and chariotry.

It spread along the Silk Road, evolving into international games like Chess, Shogi, and Xiangqi.
