Rules of War

⚜️ Raja (King): Moves 1 square in any direction.

📜 Mantri (Minister): Moves 1 square diagonally.

🐘 Gaja (Elephant): Moves 2 squares diagonally.

🐎 Ashva (Horse): Moves in an L-shape.

🛕 Ratha (Chariot): Moves straight horizontally or vertically.

⚔️ Padati (Foot-soldier): Moves 1 square straight forward.

The Heritage

Chaturanga is an ancient Indian strategy game developed during the Gupta Empire (6th century CE).

The term Chaturanga translates to the "four divisions of the military": Infantry, Cavalry, Elephantry, and Chariotry.
