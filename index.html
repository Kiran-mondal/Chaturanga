Rules of War

⚜️ Raja (King): Moves exactly 1 square in any direction. Capture the enemy Raja to win.

📜 Mantri (Minister): Moves exactly 1 square diagonally only.

🐘 Gaja (Elephant): Moves exactly 2 squares diagonally. Can jump over other pieces.

🐎 Ashva (Horse): Moves in a standard L-shape. Can jump over other pieces.

🛕 Ratha (Chariot): Moves any number of empty squares straight horizontally or vertically.

⚔️ Padati (Foot-soldier): Moves 1 square straight forward only. Captures 1 square diagonally.

The Heritage

Chaturanga is an ancient Indian strategy game developed during the Gupta Empire (6th century CE).

The term Chaturanga translates to the "four divisions of the military": Infantry, Cavalry, Elephantry, and Chariotry.

Played on an uncheckered 8x8 Ashtapada board, this epic battle of minds is widely recognized as the direct ancestor of modern games like Chess, Shogi, and Makruk.
